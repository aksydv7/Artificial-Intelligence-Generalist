# 🤖 Artificial Intelligence Generalist — 2026 Roadmap

My goal is to become an **AI Generalist** capable of understanding, building, integrating, deploying, and maintaining modern AI applications.

The focus is not on becoming a pure AI researcher, but on becoming a developer who can work across **AI, Generative AI, LLMs, AI Agents, Cloud, APIs, Automation, and Software Engineering**.

# 🎯 End Goal

By the end of this roadmap, I should be able to:

> **Design → Build → Integrate → Deploy → Evaluate → Monitor AI applications.**

I want to be capable of taking a real-world problem and deciding:

1. Do I need traditional software?
2. Do I need Machine Learning?
3. Do I need an LLM?
4. Do I need RAG?
5. Do I need an AI Agent?
6. Which model/API should I use?
7. How should I deploy it?
8. How do I secure it?
9. How do I evaluate it?
10. How do I optimize its cost and performance?
