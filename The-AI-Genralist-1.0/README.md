# The AI Genralist 1.0
AI Generalist 1.0 is designed for builders who want to master the entire AI stack, not just one slice of it. The industry no longer needs people who only know prompt engineering or only know model training. It needs generalists who can understand a business problem, pick the right AI approach (classical ML, deep learning, NLP, computer vision, generative AI, or agentic systems), build it, deploy it, and scale it. This program covers the full journey: Python and engineering foundations, classical machine learning, deep learning and neural networks, natural language processing, computer vision, generative AI and LLMs, retrieval-augmented generation, AI agents, and production deployment with cloud infrastructure. Every module builds on the last, and every module ships something real. By the end, you are not a prompt engineer. You are not a RAG developer. You are an AI Generalist who can walk into any team, assess any AI problem, and build the right solution from scratch to production.
